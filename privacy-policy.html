1. Introduction

Ludo Wordo (“the Game”) is developed and operated by Dhanadhipati Technologies LLP, Vijayawada, Andhra Pradesh, India (“Company”, “we”, “our” or “us”).

We are committed to protecting user privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how it may be shared with third-party service providers, and how users can exercise their privacy rights. It covers the Game and our website, www.ludowordo.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Personal information: user-provided names and profile pictures used for player profiles and Leaderboards, where applicable.
  • Google Sign-In information: if you choose to sign in using Google Sign-In, we may receive your Google account identifier, name, email address and profile picture, depending on the permissions requested and granted and the information made available by Google. We use this information to authenticate you, save and restore your game account, and contact you about your account where necessary.
  • Facebook Login information: if you choose to sign in using Facebook Login, we may receive your Facebook account ID, public profile name and email address, subject to the permissions granted and the information made available by Meta. We use this information to save and restore your game account and to contact you about your account. We do not sell this information.
  • Identifiers: device identifiers, including Android ID and advertising identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID (AAID), may be processed where available and permitted by device settings, applicable permissions and consent requirements. Their use depends on the services and features enabled in the Game.
  • Location data: approximate location, such as city or country, may be processed for regional Leaderboards or related features where available. We do not intentionally collect precise GPS location through the Game.
  • Usage data: gameplay activity such as levels completed, words found, coins earned, game sessions and interactions with game features may be processed to operate and improve the Game.
  • Purchase information: in-app purchases, such as coins and chapter word packs, are processed by Google Play. We receive the order ID, the item bought, the date, the amount and the purchase status. We do not receive your card, UPI or bank details.
  • Referral information: if you install the Game from a friend’s referral link, Google Play passes the referral code to the Game when it is first opened, so we can credit the invitation to the right player.
  • Meta SDK data: Ludo Wordo uses the Meta (Facebook) SDK to measure app installs and app opens so that we can evaluate the effectiveness of our advertisements on Facebook and Instagram. For this purpose, the SDK sends Meta a signal when the app is opened, together with your device’s advertising ID, where available and permitted by your device settings and applicable consent requirements. You can reset or delete your advertising ID through your phone’s settings. On supported Android devices, this may be available under Settings → Google → Ads. The available settings may vary by device and Android version.

3. How we use information

We may use information collected through the Game to:

  • Create, authenticate, maintain and restore user accounts.
  • Display player profiles and Leaderboards.
  • Save gameplay progress and provide game features.
  • Deliver purchased items, and handle refunds and payment questions.
  • Credit referral invitations.
  • Respond to account-related questions and support requests.
  • Understand gameplay activity and improve the Game.
  • Measure advertising effectiveness and app engagement.
  • Protect the Game, investigate misuse and prevent fraud.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations, including tax and accounting laws.

4. Third-party services

We use third-party service providers to support authentication, payments, analytics, advertising and other Game features. These providers may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies, settings, permissions and applicable consent requirements.

  • Google Sign-In: used as an optional sign-in method to authenticate users and help them access or restore their Ludo Wordo accounts. Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Services, Google Play Billing and Firebase: used for supported game functionality, in-app purchases, install referrals, analytics and Leaderboards, as applicable.
  • Google AdMob: used to display advertisements.
  • IronSource: used for advertising and ad mediation.
  • Unity Ads: used to display advertisements.
  • Meta (Facebook): we use the Meta SDK to measure app installs and app opens and evaluate the effectiveness of advertisements on Facebook and Instagram. We also offer Facebook Login as an optional sign-in method. If you choose Facebook Login, we may receive your Facebook account ID, public profile name and email address, subject to the permissions granted. We use this information to save and restore your game account and to contact you about your account. We do not sell this information. Meta Privacy Policy: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy

5. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information, including information collected through Google Sign-In or Facebook Login. Information may be processed by service providers to provide authentication, payments, analytics, advertising, hosting, security and other Game functions.

We may also disclose information where required by law, in response to valid legal process, or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety and security of users or the Company.

6. Data retention and deletion

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Game, maintain accounts, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes and protect our services.

Delete your account in the app: open Ludo Wordo, go to Settings → Delete account and confirm. This deletes your account and the personal information linked to it. Deleted accounts and progress cannot be recovered.

Delete by email: if you cannot open the app, email cs.dhanadhipatitechnologies@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account. Verified deletion requests are processed within 7 business days, subject to applicable legal requirements.

What we keep: records of in-app purchases (order ID, item, date and amount) are kept after deletion because Indian tax and accounting laws require it and we need them to handle refunds and disputes. We keep them only for the period those laws require. We may also keep specific information where a law, court order or an open fraud investigation requires it. The full list is on our account deletion page.

Deleting your Ludo Wordo account does not automatically delete information independently retained by Google, Meta or another third-party provider. You may need to contact that provider separately.

7. Children’s privacy

Ludo Wordo is an educational game that may appeal to students and younger users. We aim to handle children’s information in accordance with applicable laws, including India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and Google Play’s policies.

Where parental consent or other safeguards are required by applicable law, we will follow the requirements that apply to our service and the relevant user’s location.

Parents and guardians can contact us at cs.dhanadhipatitechnologies@gmail.com to ask what information we hold about their child, or to have it deleted.

8. Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we maintain against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or destruction. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your choices and privacy rights

You may choose whether to use Google Sign-In or Facebook Login. You can also reset or delete your device’s advertising ID through your device settings, where supported. On supported Android devices, advertising ID settings may be available under Settings → Google → Ads.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, object to certain processing, restrict processing, or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To make a privacy request, contact cs.dhanadhipatitechnologies@gmail.com.

10. International data processing

Some third-party service providers may process information on servers located outside your country. Where applicable, such processing is subject to the safeguards and requirements imposed by relevant data protection laws.

11. Our website

Our website, www.ludowordo.com, does not show advertisements and does not ask you to create an account.

  • Google Analytics: we use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are useful. It records information such as pages viewed, approximate location (city or country), device and browser type, and which buttons were tapped. Ad personalisation and Google signals are turned off for our website. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • YouTube videos: tutorial videos are hosted on YouTube. A video only loads from YouTube (using its privacy-enhanced mode) when you press play. YouTube then processes information under Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Referral links: the page at ludowordo.com/refer.html reads the referral code in the link and passes it to Google Play. It does not store the code.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the Game, our data practices, third-party services or applicable laws. Updates will be published on this page, and the “Last updated” date will be revised accordingly.
