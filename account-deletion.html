Delete your account

Delete your Ludo Wordo account and data, right from the app.

This page explains how to delete your account in Ludo Wordo (Google Play developer: Dhanadhipati Technologies LLP). You can delete your account and the data linked to it at any time, directly from the app. You do not need to contact us.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Ludo Wordo and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Read the message and confirm.

After you confirm, you are signed out and your account is deleted. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Cannot open the app? Email cs.dhanadhipatitechnologies@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account, with the subject Account Deletion Request. Include your username or user ID. We verify that the account is yours and delete it within 7 business days, then confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: username, display name and profile picture.
  • Your sign-in details: the Google or Facebook account ID and email address linked to your account.
  • Your game data: progress, coins, rewards, House of Words collection and any word packs unlocked on the account.
  • Your friends list, friend requests, referral code and referral history.
  • Your leaderboard entries.

Coins, word packs and other items on a deleted account cannot be restored or moved to another account.

What we keep, and why

  • Purchase records. For in-app purchases we keep the order ID, the item bought, the date and the amount. Indian tax and accounting laws require us to keep these records, and we also need them to handle refunds and payment disputes. They are kept only for as long as those laws require and are not used for anything else.
  • Records we must keep by law. If a law, court order or an open investigation into fraud or misuse requires us to keep specific information, we keep only that information and only for as long as required.
  • Statistics that no longer identify you. Counts such as the number of matches played may remain in our reports, but they cannot be linked back to you.

Data held by other companies

Deleting your Ludo Wordo account does not delete information that other companies hold about you on their own:

  • Google Play keeps its own record of your purchases. See Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • If you signed in with Google or Facebook, you can remove Ludo Wordo from the connected apps in your Google or Facebook account settings.
  • You can reset your phone’s advertising ID in your device settings (on many Android phones: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Security & privacy → Ads).

Questions

Email cs.dhanadhipatitechnologies@gmail.com. You can also read our Privacy Policy.
