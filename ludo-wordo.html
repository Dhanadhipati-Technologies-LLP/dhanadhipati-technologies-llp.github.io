Game guide

Everything you need to know to win your first Ludo Wordo match.

The goal

Each match starts with one secret target word, your Word Quest. Some of its letters are missing. Collect the missing letters from the Ludo path and place them in the word. The first player to complete the word wins instantly.

It is not a race to the home square. There is no finish point: pawns keep moving until someone completes the word, so strategy and smart positioning matter more than speed.

Moving and collecting letters

  • Move your pawns by the number shown on the dice.
  • Every square on the path holds a letter. Land on a square to collect its letter.
  • Your collected letters are stored in Your Vault.

The Collections screen

Tap the big button in the centre of the board (the home area in classic Ludo) to open it.

  • Word Quest: your target word, with its missing letters.
  • Your Vault: the letters you have collected.
  • Rivals’ Vault: the letters each opponent has collected, in their colour.

Check it often. Knowing what your opponents hold tells you whom to chase.

Placing letters

  1. Drag a collected letter to a blank in the target word.
  2. If it is right, it locks into place and every player sees it.
  3. If it is wrong, it goes back to your collection.

Pawn battles

Knock out a rival’s pawn and you take one letter from their vault. A well-timed capture can win the match, and your captures are counted on the results screen.

Clues

Cannot guess the word? Use a clue:

  • Meaning clue: read what the word means.
  • Speech clue: hear how the word sounds.

Unlock a clue with a Meaning or Speech card, with coins, or by watching a short video. You can win clue cards from the Mystery Box.

Winning

Complete the word first and you win. The results screen shows the coins you won, your rank, your capture count, and the word with its meaning, so every win teaches you a new word.

Game modes

  • Multiplayer: real-time matches with 2 to 4 players online, on a mini or maxi board. Bots fill any empty seats.
  • Play with friends: create a private room and send invites.

Coins and rewards

  • Each match has an entry fee in coins. Winning earns you more coins.
  • Collect a daily reward each day you log in.
  • Spin the wheel for extra rewards when you run low on coins.
  • Open the Mystery Box for Meaning and Speech cards.
  • Complete missions for more rewards.

Coins are virtual. They have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money.

7 words a week

Every week you get 7 connected words built around one root word. Day 1 introduces the root and the first word, and each of the next 6 days adds one more.

Each word comes with its meaning, a memory trick, an example sentence, part of speech, synonyms, antonyms and grammar notes. Understand the root and all 7 words are easier to remember.

CBSE and State board word packs

Word packs bring chapter words from Class 6 to 10 textbooks, in subjects such as Science and Social Science, into your matches. Buy them in the in-app store.

House of Words

Every word you win with is added to your House of Words, your own vocabulary collection to revisit any time.

Leaderboards

Climb the global leaderboards with weekly, monthly and seasonal rankings. Top players earn rewards.

Friends and referrals

  • Add friends by username, and send or accept friend requests.
  • Share your referral link to invite friends to the game.
