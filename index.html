How to play

It plays like the Ludo you already know. The difference is what you are racing for: not the home square, but the letters of a hidden word.

  1. Pick a match

    Play online with 2–4 players on a mini or maxi board, or open a private room and invite friends. Bots fill any empty seats.

  2. See the target word

    Each match starts with a secret word, your Word Quest. Some of its letters are missing.

  3. Roll and collect

    Move your pawns by the dice roll. Every square you land on adds its letter to Your Vault.

  4. Place your letters

    Drag a letter into a blank in the word. A right letter locks in; a wrong one goes back to your collection.

  5. Capture to steal

    Knock out a rival’s pawn and take one letter from their vault. Stuck? Use a Meaning or Speech card.

  6. Finish the word, win

    The first player to complete the word wins the match and the coins in the pot.

7 words a week

Every week the game picks one root word and unlocks one new word built from it each day. Each word comes with its meaning, a memory trick, an example sentence, part of speech, synonyms and antonyms.

In the week shown here the root is log/logy, meaning “word, study, reason”. Learn the root once and logic, logical and the rest of the week’s words become easier to remember.

Questions

Is Ludo Wordo free?

Yes, it is free to download and play. Optional in-app purchases are available, such as coins and chapter word packs.

Is this real-money gaming?

No. Entry fees and prizes are paid in in-game coins. Coins and other virtual items have no real-world value and cannot be exchanged for cash.

What are Meaning and Speech cards?

They are clue cards. A Meaning card reveals what the secret word means, and a Speech card plays how it sounds. You can win them from the Mystery Box, or unlock a clue with coins or by watching a short video.

Which classes do the word packs cover?

Packs follow CBSE and State board chapters for Class 6 to 10, in subjects such as Science and Social Science. You can see every available pack in the in-app store.

Can I play with my friends?

Yes. Create a private room inside the game and invite your friends. You can also add friends by username.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Ludo Wordo is available for Android on Google Play. An iPhone version is planned.

How does a referral link work?

Share your referral link from the game. When a friend opens it on Android, it takes them to Google Play with your code attached, so the game can credit the invite after they install.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Settings and tap Delete account. The account deletion page explains what is deleted and what is kept.
